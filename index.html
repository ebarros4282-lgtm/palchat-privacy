PalChat Communicator Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Contact: mypalcommunications@gmail.com

PalChat Communicator is a communication app. A person taps pictures and words to build a sentence, and the device speaks it aloud. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does with information. The short version: PalChat keeps your boards, words, photos and settings on your device. It has no accounts, and we do not collect your information. Only one thing uses the internet, and it asks a caregiver first: the natural voices that speak the words. It is described in full below.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores the following only on your device:

  • the communication boards and the words and pictures on them;
  • words, folders and photos a caregiver adds (a photo is only used if a caregiver picks one from the photo library or takes one with the camera);
  • profile names or nicknames a caregiver types, and the answers to the optional set-up questions (where the app will be used, the person's communication stage, access needs such as bigger buttons or a calmer layout, and any notes a caregiver types). These only choose which words and layout are shown, and never leave the device;
  • settings such as voice, volume, and which words are shown, and the caregiver's choice about the natural voices;
  • audio of the board's words and of spoken phrases in the chosen voice, so they play instantly and work without internet.

We do not receive any of this. It is not uploaded, synced, backed up by us, or shared with us or anyone else. If the device itself is backed up by the device maker's own backup service, the app's data may be included in that backup under that service's terms.

What the app does not do

  • There are no accounts, no sign-up and no sign-in.
  • There is no cloud sync.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app contains no third-party advertising or analytics software.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • The app does not use the microphone. It does not read the contact list, location or other apps' data.

Speech

PalChat speaks with natural-sounding voices (Sophie, Luke, Alex, Jessica or Brian, chosen per profile in Voice settings). These voices are made online. The first time PalChat is used on a device, it asks a caregiver whether to turn them on and shows exactly what is sent. Until a caregiver agrees, or if they choose "Not now", PalChat speaks with the voice built into the device and no words are sent anywhere for speaking. A caregiver can change this at any time in Voice settings, and "Erase all data" resets it.

What the natural voices send. When they are on, PalChat sends only words to be spoken, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), to PalChat's voice service. The service asks a text-to-speech provider (ElevenLabs) to turn them into audio and returns it. Those words are the words on the board and a few common phrases (fetched in the background, so buttons speak instantly and also work without internet), and any sentence or idea that is spoken (for example: I want more). Nothing else is sent: no name, no photos, no profile information, no device identifiers, no account. This data is not linked to anyone, is not used for tracking or advertising, and is used only to produce the speech.

What is kept, and where.

  • On the device. The audio is saved on the device so words play instantly and without internet. "Erase all data" removes it.
  • On the voice service. The voice service is a small program run by us on a third-party hosting company, which handles the request on our behalf. To make repeats faster, the service keeps the audio for short words and phrases (up to 40 characters) in memory for up to a day. It is stored under a scrambled code (a hash), so the words are not kept as text. It is never written to disk, is not linked to anyone, and is lost whenever the service restarts. The service does not log the words.
  • With the provider. The provider receives the words only to produce the audio and may keep them briefly under its own policy.
  • Network address. As with any internet request, the service can see the device's network address while it answers. A short-lived counter by address is kept in memory for about a minute to limit abuse and is never written to disk.

Never silent. If a word's audio is not on the device and cannot be fetched quickly (for example, with no internet), the device's built-in voice speaks the same words.

Do not make buttons whose words are a child's full name, address or other private details. The board's words are sent so they can be spoken.

Photos and camera

The photo library or camera is opened only when a caregiver chooses to add a picture to a button, and the system asks for permission first. The chosen picture is stored on the device only. You can turn the permission off at any time in the device's Settings.

Printing and sharing

"Print home board" makes a printable page of the board on the device and hands it to the system print or share sheet. What happens next (printing, saving, sending) is up to the person using the sheet. The app does not send it anywhere.

Sentence ideas

Sentence ideas offers short, well-formed suggestions based on the words in the sentence bar (for example, "I want eat" becomes "I want to eat."). They are made on the device by a built-in rule-based tool. Nothing leaves the device and ideas work without internet. A caregiver can switch ideas off, per profile, in Settings. The app has no online sentence or AI-suggestion feature.

Children

PalChat is made to be used by children together with a parent, caregiver, teacher or therapist. The app does not ask a child for personal information, has no accounts, and does not collect information from children. Settings, editing and printing are in a caregiver area that needs a press-and-hold to open, so they are not opened by accident.

Deleting your data

Because everything is on the device, you control it. In Settings you can remove profiles, words and photos you added, or erase everything the app stores on the device. Deleting the app also removes its data from the device. There is nothing on our side to delete, because we do not hold your data.

Security

Data stays in the app's private storage on the device and is protected by the device's own security (passcode and system encryption). The natural voices use an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The key used to reach the voice provider is kept only on the server and is never part of the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If the way the natural voices work changes, the app will ask a caregiver to agree again before they are used.
