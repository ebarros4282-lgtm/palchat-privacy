Privacy Policy

PalChat Communicator (iOS / iPadOS)
Last updated: 8 September 2026
Operator: MyPal Communications
Contact: mypalcommunications@gmail.com

PalChat Communicator is an AAC (augmentative and alternative communication) app that helps people build messages with picture tiles and speak them aloud. Core communication features work on your device and do not require an account. Some optional features use the internet.

1. Information stored on your device

  • Vocabulary boards, tiles, folders, layout, and profile settings
  • Optional custom tile images you choose from your photo library or take with the camera
  • Voice preferences
  • Optional setup answers you enter for a profile

This information is stored locally on the device. Custom photos are used only as tile artwork you create.

2. Optional Suggest AI

If you use Suggest while online, the app sends the words currently in your sentence strip to our sentence-suggestion service solely to return suggested completions. We do not use this content for advertising. Short-term server logs may be kept for reliability and security and are deleted within 30 days. We do not sell this data.

3. Optional account and cloud sync

Creating an account is optional. If you sign up or sign in, we process your email address and authentication credentials through our cloud provider (Supabase) to secure your account, and we may sync your profile data (boards, tiles, settings, and related saved content) across devices signed into the same account. You can use the app without signing in.

4. Information we do not collect in the current app

As of this policy, PalChat does not request microphone access for recording, does not use your location, and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs for tracking you across other companies’ apps or websites. Speech is produced with on-device text-to-speech.

5. How we use information

  • Provide AAC boards, speech, and customisation
  • Optionally suggest sentence completions when you ask for them
  • Optionally sync your profiles across your devices when you sign in
  • Respond to support requests you send us

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Sharing

We use service providers that process data on our behalf for optional features, including Supabase (authentication and optional sync) and Render (Suggest AI hosting). We do not share app content with advertisers.

7. Children’s privacy

PalChat may be used by children with caregiver, educator, or clinician support. Optional account features are intended for caregivers. Please do not create an account in a child’s name with a child’s personal email if a caregiver account can be used instead. To exercise privacy rights on behalf of a child, contact us at the email above.

8. Retention

  • On-device data remains until you delete it in the app or remove the app
  • Account and synced data: while the account is active
  • Suggest AI logs: up to 30 days

To request deletion of an optional cloud account and synced profiles, email mypalcommunications@gmail.com from the address associated with the account.

9. Security

We use reputable providers and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Your choices

  • Use the app without signing in
  • Decline camera or photo library permission (custom photo tiles will be limited)
  • Avoid Suggest or use the device offline
  • Sign out or request account deletion via email

11. International processing

Optional cloud features may process data on servers outside your country (for example via Supabase or Render).

12. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the revised version on this page with an updated date.
