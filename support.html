PalChat Communicator is a picture-and-word communication board. A person taps pictures to build a sentence, and the app speaks it aloud. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Getting started

  • Open the app and answer the short set-up questions (you can skip them).
  • Tap pictures to build a sentence, then tap Speak. Tap a folder (a picture with a small folder mark) to see more words.
  • The Ideas strip under the sentence suggests short, complete sentences. Tap an idea to hear it. Ideas are made on the device and work without internet.

Caregiver settings

Settings are kept away from accidental taps: press and hold the gear button for about 1.5 seconds. There you can switch profiles, change the voice, hide or show words, rearrange buttons, print the home board, read the caregiver guide, and erase all data on the device.

Voices

  • PalChat speaks with five natural voices: Sophie (the default), Luke, Alex, Jessica and Brian. Choose one per profile in Settings, then Voice and sound.
  • The natural voices are made online. The first time, PalChat asks a caregiver before turning them on and lists exactly what is sent. If you choose Not now, the device's built-in voice speaks and nothing is sent.
  • The board's words are saved on the device in the chosen voice, so they speak instantly and also work without internet. If a word cannot be fetched (for example, a new button while offline), the built-in voice says it, so the person is never left without a voice.
  • No sound? Check the volume and the device's mute setting, then try again.

Privacy

Everything you add stays on the device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Read the full privacy policy.

Erasing data

In caregiver settings, choose Erase all data on this device. This removes every profile, board, word, picture you added, setting and saved voice audio. Deleting the app also removes its data.

Not medical advice

PalChat Communicator is a communication tool. It does not diagnose, treat or replace advice from a speech-language professional.
