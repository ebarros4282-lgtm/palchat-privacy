PalChat Communicator support

PalChat Communicator is a picture-and-word communication board. Tap pictures to build a sentence, then press Speak.

Contact: mypalcommunications@gmail.com. Please say which phone or tablet and which version of PalChat you use. Do not send photos or names of people in an email unless you want to.

Common questions

How do I open Settings?

Tap the gear button at the top right.

How do I hide or show words, or move buttons?

Open Settings, then Edit board. Tap a button to select it, then use Hide or Show, or the Move buttons. You can also drag a button to another place. Use Add to make your own word or folder, with a photo or a picture from the built-in set.

How do I change or turn off the natural voices?

Open Settings, then Voice and sound. Choose Sophie, Luke, Alex, Jessica or Brian, or press “Stop using natural voices on this device”. With natural voices off, the device's built-in voice speaks and no words are sent anywhere.

Why does a word sometimes sound different?

If a word's natural-voice audio is not on the device yet and cannot be fetched quickly (for example, with no internet), the device's built-in voice speaks so the board is never silent. Once the audio has been fetched, the natural voice is used.

How do I print the board?

Open Settings, then Print home board. The system print or share sheet opens.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings, then Erase all data on this device. This removes every profile, board, word, photo copy, setting and saved voice choice. Deleting the app also removes its data. PalChat has no accounts and keeps nothing on its own servers.
